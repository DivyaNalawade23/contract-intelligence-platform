SAMPLE VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT (FICTIONAL TRAINING TEXT)

This fictional sample is for software testing and is not a real legal contract.

1. Term and Renewal
This agreement will automatically renew for one-year renewal terms unless either party
provides written notice before the renewal date.

2. Liability
The supplier accepts unlimited liability for all claims arising under this agreement.

3. Data Security
The parties will follow applicable security practices. The contract should be reviewed to
confirm whether it adequately specifies what happens after a data breach.

4. Confidentiality
Each party will protect confidential information received from the other party.

5. Indemnification
The supplier will indemnify and hold harmless the customer for specified third-party claims.

6. Termination
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing written notice as described in the
final signed agreement.
